Background

I'm Veenu Chhabra, a Master's student at IIT Bombay (Roll No. 24M2135) in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, working with Prof. Abir De on my thesis about over-squashing in Graph Neural Networks — why graph topology creates information bottlenecks and how to fix them.

My work spans theoretical ML (graph curvature, spectral methods, effective resistance) and hardcore systems engineering: I've built distributed key-value stores, Unix shells, and a Docker-like container runtime from the ground up — all in C/C++.

I also work with Dolby Laboratories on an automated pipeline that turns raw video into graph-structured datasets using Vision-Language Models. Previously, I graduated as Department Rank 1 in B.Tech IT and ranked Top 10 in IIT Bombay's ML Kaggle competition.

Research & Collaboration

M.S. by Research Thesis
MAR 2026 — PRESENT
Understanding and Mitigating Over-Squashing in Graph Neural Networks
Guide: Dr. Abir De · Department of CSE, IIT Bombay
  • Investigating over-squashing — how graph topology and message-passing create information bottlenecks that restrict long-range propagation and limit GNN expressiveness.
  • Analyzing bottlenecks through effective resistance, graph curvature, spectral graph theory, and Jacobian sensitivity metrics to characterize structural failure modes.
  • Exploring task-aware diagnostics, graph rewiring strategies, and topology-aware architectures to improve information propagation on diverse graph learning benchmarks.

Video-to-Graph Dataset Generation using Vision-Language Models
Collaboration with Dolby Laboratories · Guide: Dr. Abir De
  • Built an automated pipeline for generating graph-structured video datasets using VLMs — extracting entities, relationships, temporal events, and hierarchical scene info from raw video sequences.
  • Designed a multi-stage framework incorporating prompt decomposition, response aggregation, conflict resolution, and object grounding to convert VLM outputs into structured graph representations.
  • Evaluated Qwen-VL, DeepSeek-VL2, GroundingDINO, and YOLO to improve semantic extraction quality while significantly reducing reliance on manual annotation.
